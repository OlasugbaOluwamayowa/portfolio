About me

I'm Olasugba Oluwamayowa Akinmade, a Physiology graduate with a B.Sc. from the University of Ibadan. My work sits where the clinic meets the keyboard: I record and report real patient data, then look for ways to make that process faster, clearer and easier to learn from.

I have hands-on diagnostic experience in ECG, EEG, EMG and nerve conduction studies, and I've led health outreach teams of doctors, nurses, physiologists and pharmacists as a community health leader.

Outside the hospital, I'm teaching myself Python, SQL and modern web development, with a growing interest in on-chain analytics and automation.
